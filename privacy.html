1. Introduction

NovaX Apps built LifeLens: Voice & Vision Guide as an AI-powered accessibility and visual assistance app. It helps people identify objects, read documents and labels, understand medicines, recognise hazards and hear explanations.

LifeLens has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics SDK. NovaX Apps does not operate a backend server that receives or stores your photos, voice, scans, chat messages or personal details. This policy explains what happens on your device and when data is sent directly to a third-party service.

Short version: your history and settings stay on your phone. When you use cloud AI, your image or text is sent directly from the app to Google's Gemini API over HTTPS. We do not receive it. You can delete local data in the app or by uninstalling LifeLens.

2. Information the App Accesses

Camera and images

  • Permission: android.permission.CAMERA. The camera hardware is optional; you can use the gallery picker instead.
  • Used for: capturing a photo when you tap Scan, or an image you choose from your gallery.
  • Cloud processing: when cloud AI is used, the image is downscaled on your device (longest side up to 1024 pixels) and sent over encrypted HTTPS/TLS directly to the Google Gemini API to generate the answer. NovaX Apps does not operate a relay and does not receive your image.
  • On-device processing: if you download the optional on-device AI, the image can be analysed locally and no image leaves your phone.
  • Retention: NovaX Apps does not receive, store or sell your images. Google processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Offline queue: a scan captured while offline is stored on your device and analysed later when cloud AI or the on-device model is available.

Microphone and voice

  • Permission: android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO.
  • Used for: hearing a spoken question or voice command when you tap the microphone or use the voice agent.
  • Processing: speech is converted to text by Android's standard speech recognition service. Depending on your device and settings, that service may process audio on Google's servers. If you download the optional Whisper offline voice model, transcription happens entirely on your phone.
  • Retention: NovaX Apps does not receive, store or upload your audio recordings or the resulting transcript. If you send a voice question to cloud AI, the transcript and your question go directly to Google.

Live companion and chat

The Live companion can name objects and warn about hazards. It uses the on-device vision model when available; when online, a throttled cloud explanation may also be requested. The Chat tab is answered by cloud Gemini when online or by the optional on-device model. Chat conversations and chat photos are stored locally on your device so you can reopen them.

When you choose to report an AI response, LifeLens opens your email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you review and send the email yourself. The report contains the response text, your optional comment, the app version, Android version and device locale.

Local storage on your device

  • Language, voice speed, auto-speak, proxy configuration and other preferences.
  • The optional display name you give for greetings.
  • Scan history: the latest explanations, stored as text on this device.
  • Pending scans waiting for cloud AI or an on-device model.
  • Chat conversations and chat photos.
  • Medicine cabinet: name, purpose and expiry date.
  • Reminders and their alarm schedules.
  • Emergency Card: medical ID details, encrypted at rest with the Android Keystore.
  • Cached translations and custom API or proxy settings, if you enter any.
  • Downloaded on-device AI and offline voice model files.

Device and technical information

LifeLens contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. It does not collect advertising IDs, precise location, contacts or your installed-app list. When cloud AI is used, Google receives the image or text you submitted and standard request metadata such as your IP address, app/API client information and request time under Google's own privacy policy. NovaX Apps does not add an install identifier and does not run a server that receives these requests.

Notifications and alarms

With android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, LifeLens can show medicine reminders and scheduled-scan alerts. With android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, it can deliver reminders at the time you choose on Android 12 and later. These permissions are used only for features you enable, never for advertising.

Barcodes, QR codes, sharing and links

Barcodes and QR codes are decoded on your device. If you open a link, share an explanation, share the Emergency Card or send a report, Android hands the data to the app you choose. That app or website then applies its own privacy policy.

4. On-Device AI and Offline Voice Models

If you download the optional on-device AI or offline voice models, their files are fetched over HTTPS from Hugging Face and run entirely on your phone. Examples include Qwen2.5 (Apache-2.0) and Whisper (MIT). After download, offline scan explanation, offline chat, offline voice transcription, barcode decoding and reading the Emergency Card happen without sending data to NovaX Apps. You can delete either model in Settings.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Scan history: open Saved Scans and clear it.
  • Chat history and chat photos: open Chat history and choose Clear all chats.
  • Medicines and reminders: remove each item in the app.
  • Emergency Card: clear the fields and save.
  • Pending scans and translations: cleared by the in-app reset or by clearing app data.
  • All local data: Android Settings, Apps, LifeLens, Storage, Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Email requests: support emails remain in the mailbox until you or the publisher delete them.

NovaX Apps holds no server-side account or database from which to delete app content. Data already processed by Google or Hugging Face is governed by their retention policies; you can use their account and privacy controls to manage it.

7. AI Accuracy, Medical and Safety Disclaimer

Medical and safety disclaimer:
LifeLens is an informational accessibility tool. It is not a doctor, pharmacist, emergency service, lawyer or certified safety inspector. AI can be wrong, misread labels, or miss hazards. Never change medication doses or delay emergency care based on the app. Always verify medicine details with a licensed professional.

Guidance about chemicals, electricity, machinery, vehicles, farming and first aid is informational only. Follow the product label and official guidance, and call your local emergency number when in doubt.

8. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit with HTTPS/TLS for cloud AI and model downloads. Cleartext HTTP is disabled.
  • The Emergency Card and any custom API or proxy secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and keys held by the Android Keystore.
  • Screenshots and recent-app previews are blocked while sensitive screens are open, including the Emergency Card, medicine cabinet, chat history, cloud settings, privacy policy and terms.
  • Android backup is disabled for the app, and sensitive preferences are excluded from backup and device transfer.
  • The app performs a signature-integrity check before sending cloud requests and can refuse cloud access in a modified build or hostile environment.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Use a device lock and keep Android updated.

9. Publisher-Supplied Cloud API Key

Cloud features use a Google Gemini API key supplied by the publisher, so you do not need your own account. The key is a technical credential, not personal data. Do not extract, publish, share or abuse it. Abuse can be blocked, rate-limited or reported. If you configure your own API key or self-hosted proxy, you are responsible for that service and its terms.

10. Children's Privacy
