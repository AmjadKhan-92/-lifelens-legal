Key points

  • No account, sign-in, advertising or analytics SDK.
  • NovaX Apps does not operate a server that receives your photos, voice, scans or personal details.
  • Cloud AI requests go directly from the app to the Google Gemini API over HTTPS/TLS.
  • The optional on-device AI and Whisper voice model run entirely on your phone after download.
  • Scan history, chat history, chat photos, medicines, reminders and the Emergency Card are stored locally on your device.
  • You can delete local data from inside the app or by uninstalling LifeLens.
Safety notice: LifeLens is not a doctor, pharmacist, emergency service or certified safety inspector. AI can be wrong. Never delay emergency care or change medicine doses based on the app. Call your local emergency number when needed.
